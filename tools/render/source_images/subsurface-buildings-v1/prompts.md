# Subsurface building sprite prompts v1

Built-in image generation was used with the existing oblique building atlases as style,
palette, scale, and camera references only. Every normal sprite was generated against a flat
`#00FF00` background, then its winter sprite was derived as a geometry-preserving edit.

## Shared direction

- Historical late-Joseon Korean frontier settlement simulation building sprite
- Clean warm-brown HD pixel-painted raster with restrained dark outlines
- Inclined orthographic top-down view, compact footprint, bottom-center anchor
- Full object visible with generous padding, no floor tile or contact shadow
- Perfectly uniform `#00FF00` chroma-key background
- No people, scenery, text, watermark, or bright green in the subject

## Well

One-tile low open Korean village well: irregular pale-gray granite curb, dark water opening,
plain flat cover planks, hemp rope coil, and an unadorned wooden water pail. No roof, gable,
posts, crossbeam, pulley, crank, or European wishing-well silhouette.

Winter edit: preserve exact geometry and add restrained snow caps to upper granite faces,
planks, rope, and pail rim while keeping the open water readable.

## Surface mine camp

One-tile outdoor ore collection and sorting camp: low open-sided timber lean-to with a simple
shed roof, sorted gray stone and reddish iron ore piles, woven baskets, sorting table, hand
tools, and stacked timbers. No tunnel, cave, dark doorway, shaft, portal, rails, cart, or
industrial machinery.

Winter edit: preserve exact geometry and add light accumulation to the roof, timbers, basket
rims, and pile tops while retaining the ore colors and tools.

## Deep mine

Two-by-two permanent Korean underground adit: black horizontal tunnel mouth in a low
rock-and-earth embankment, heavy rectangular timber portal and internal cribbing, retaining
stones, replacement support logs, woven ore baskets, and small sorted ore piles. No rails,
cart, machinery, Western sign, masonry arch, or fantasy crystals.

Winter edit: preserve exact geometry and add uneven snow to the embankment, upper rocks,
lintel, logs, baskets, and pile tops while keeping the tunnel mouth dominant.
