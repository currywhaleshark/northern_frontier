# Rainwater cistern ImageGen prompts

Built with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-08-05. The normal frame
used the existing folk-building, saltworks, and tidal-fishery sheets as style
references. The winter frame was an edit of the normal frame. The committed
alpha sources were produced from flat green chroma renders with the imagegen
skill's `remove_chroma_key.py` helper.

## Normal

Create one compact late-Joseon Korean rainwater cistern utility sprite for a
1x1 ground tile: a low wooden shingled lean-to roof with a readable rain gutter
and short spout feeding two or three brown onggi storage jars on a low irregular
stone foundation. Match the repository's warm oblique pixel-art building style.
Use a perfectly flat `#00ff00` chroma-key background with no floor, shadow,
text, people, scenery, or extra panels.

## Winter edit

Preserve the normal frame's exact geometry, camera, proportions, placements,
scale, framing, pixel-art resolution, and chroma background. Add restrained
snow caps to the roof, gutter edge, jar rims, and a few stones. Freeze the
visible jar water to pale blue-gray with a thin icy rim and remove the falling
water stream. Change nothing else.
