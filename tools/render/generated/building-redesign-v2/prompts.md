# Oblique building redesign v2

Built-in ImageGen was used with `camera-reference.png` as the mandatory
projection/style reference. All final normal assets share these camera rules:

- inclined orthographic top-down, almost frontal;
- broad front facade with only a shallow right side visible;
- horizontal roof/platform front edges and perfectly vertical posts;
- no perspective convergence;
- bottom-center anchor and the same 2x2 footprint envelope as the existing
  oblique building atlas;
- flat solid `#FF00FF` background, no ground patch or cast shadow.

## 당집 (`shrine`)

Small Korean village guardian shrine: straw-thatched timber-and-earth room,
open doors, a mountain-spirit panel, plain spirit tablets, a low altar,
irregular stone cairn, and an offering stone. Explicitly exclude shimenawa,
zigzag paper, gohei, torii, Shinto bells, and Japanese lanterns.

## 암자 (`hermitage`)

Compact late-Joseon Buddhist hall with a dark tiled roof, restrained faded
dancheong, fully open central doors, and one large readable seated gilt Buddha
on a red-brown altar with a halo and two candles. One small stone lantern stays
close to the foundation.

## 봉수대 (`beacon`)

Broad rough circular drystone yeondae on a low defensive footing, low upper
parapet, one shallow signal hearth with stacked wood and a tightly contained
flame, simple timber ladder, base windbreak stones, and a firewood bundle.
Explicitly exclude classical, Greek/Roman, stepped-pyramid, and European
castle forms.

## 불랑기포대 (`cannonEmplacement`)

Tall roofless timber artillery watchtower on stone footings, four braced legs,
a high square firing deck with timber and packed-earth mantlets, broad
stair-ladder, one elevated iron bulwark cannon running diagonally from the
rear-left toward the front-right opening, and one closed ammunition chest. The
horizontal deck is strongly foreshortened into a shallow orthographic
trapezoid rather than a square bird's-eye platform. Explicitly exclude
ground-level batteries, European castle towers, fantasy machinery, smoke, and
muzzle flash.

## Winter variants

Each winter image is an edit of its final normal image. Geometry, camera,
silhouette, scale, and object positions are locked. Only restrained snow on
upward-facing surfaces and cooler exposed highlights are added; interiors,
flame, Buddha, cannon, and access paths remain readable.
